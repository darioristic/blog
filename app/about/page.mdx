I’m Dario Ristić, a technology executive, entrepreneur, consultant, and
product builder from Belgrade, Serbia. Today, I lead **Platforma**, a technology
research, consulting, and product development company focused on researching,
designing, and building AI-native services, platforms, digital products, and
hardware-enabled solutions.

My career has been shaped by Linux and open source. Long before cloud computing
became mainstream, I was fascinated by the idea that powerful technology could
be built, shared, and improved by communities rather than controlled by a
handful of companies. As a teenager, I advocated for Linux adoption, helped
people around me build and run open source systems, and became deeply involved
in the communities around them.

That early passion eventually became a career in building infrastructure and
cloud platforms. I co-founded **SezamPro Hosting** and **Ehost**, two of
Serbia’s early managed hosting companies, and later **CloudEx**, one of the
country’s early OpenStack-based cloud providers. **Platforma** itself began as
my first venture - a small digital agency that I eventually brought back, this
time with a new mission centered on AI, emerging technologies, and product
innovation.

This includes the **research, design, implementation, and development of
AI-powered digital products and platforms**, as well as **AI-enabled hardware
and physical products**. My work spans the entire journey from early research
and experimentation to architecture, proof of concept, engineering, deployment,
and scaling. The goal is not simply to adopt AI, but to understand where it
creates meaningful value and build the technology required to deliver it.

Throughout my career, I’ve worked at the intersection of technology, product,
and business. At **SBB – United Group**, I led the Cloud Department, where we
built one of Serbia’s early cloud platforms. At **Comtrade System
Integration**, I led innovation and business development, working on projects
involving artificial intelligence, distributed systems, and event-driven
architectures.

I also co-founded and led **Cloud Native d.o.o.**, a cloud-native technology
consultancy focused on Kubernetes, OpenShift, cloud platforms, and modern
infrastructure. At **Propulsion App**, I was involved in product development,
and I have advised organizations such as **Orion Telekom** on technology
strategy, cloud, and digital transformation.

My current focus is increasingly on **AI-native architecture and AI product
engineering** - understanding how AI changes the way we build software,
platforms, infrastructure, devices, and businesses. I’m particularly interested
in the convergence of AI, cloud-native technologies, robotics, intelligent
hardware, automation, developer platforms, and open source.

Outside of building companies and technology platforms, I speak at conferences
and industry events about AI-native and cloud-native architecture, DevOps, open
source, and the future of technology. I’ve also spent years supporting the
developer and open source communities in Serbia.

Most of what I know was taught to me by people who were willing to share what
they knew - through documentation, forums, conferences, code, and simply taking
the time to help someone else. That culture of openness has shaped both how I
build technology and how I approach my work.

**I believe the future belongs to people and organizations that can move quickly
from research to experimentation, from experimentation to products, and from
products to real-world impact.**
