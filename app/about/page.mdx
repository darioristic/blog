I’m Dario Ristić, a technology executive, entrepreneur, consultant, and product
builder. I lead **Platforma**, a technology research, consulting, and product
development company focused on **AI-native technologies, digital products,
platforms, and intelligent hardware**.

My career began with Linux and open source. As a teenager, I advocated for open
source adoption and helped others build and run Linux systems. That passion
evolved into building some of Serbia’s early hosting and cloud platforms. I
co-founded **SezamPro Hosting, Ehost, and CloudEx**, one of the country’s early
OpenStack-based cloud providers.

Throughout my career, I’ve led cloud, innovation, product, and technology
initiatives at organizations including **SBB - United Group** and **Comtrade
System Integration**. I also co-founded **Cloud Native d.o.o.**, a cloud-native
consultancy, and have worked with companies on technology strategy, AI, cloud,
and digital transformation.

Today, through Platforma, I work as a consultant and builder across the full
technology lifecycle - from **AI research and experimentation to architecture,
prototyping, implementation, and product development**. I help organizations
turn emerging technologies into real products, including **AI-powered software,
platforms, and hardware solutions**.

My focus is the convergence of **AI, cloud-native infrastructure, intelligent
hardware, automation, and open source** - and how these technologies can be
transformed into products that create real business value.

I also speak at conferences about AI-native and cloud-native architecture,
DevOps, open source, and the future of technology.
