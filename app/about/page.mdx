I’m Dario Ristić, a technology executive, entrepreneur, consultant, and product
builder. I lead **Platforma**, a technology research and product company focused
on **AI-native systems, digital products, platforms, and intelligent hardware**.

My career started with Linux and open source and evolved into building some of
Serbia’s early hosting and cloud platforms. I co-founded **SezamPro Hosting,
Ehost, and CloudEx**, one of the country’s early OpenStack cloud providers.

I’ve led cloud, innovation, product, and technology initiatives at **SBB -
United Group** and **Comtrade System Integration**, and co-founded **Cloud
Native d.o.o.**, a cloud-native technology consultancy.

Today, through Platforma, I work with organizations on **AI research, technology
strategy, architecture, implementation, and product development** - turning
emerging technologies into production-ready digital and hardware products.

My focus is the convergence of **AI, cloud-native infrastructure, intelligent
hardware, automation, and open source**.

I speak at conferences about AI-native architecture, cloud-native technologies,
DevOps, open source, and the future of technology.

**I turn emerging technology into products, platforms, and businesses that work
in the real world.**
