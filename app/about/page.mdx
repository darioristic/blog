I'm Dario Ristić, a technology executive and entrepreneur from Belgrade,
Serbia. I run Platforma, a consulting and development agency for AI-native
services and platforms.

I owe most of my career to the Linux and open source communities.

In my early teens I advocated for Linux adoption and taught people around me
how to run open source systems. That turned into a career building some of the
first cloud platforms in Serbia.

I co-founded SezamPro Hosting and Ehost, two of Serbia's early managed hosting
companies, and later CloudEx, one of the first OpenStack-based cloud providers
in the country. Platforma itself started as my first venture, a small digital
agency — I've since brought it back with a new focus on AI.

At SBB – United Group I directed the Cloud Department, where we built one of
the first cloud platforms in Serbia. At Comtrade System Integration I led
innovation and business development, working on projects in artificial
intelligence and event-driven architectures. I co-founded and ran Cloud Native
d.o.o., a cloud-native consultancy, led product development at Propulsion App,
and advised Orion Telekom on technology strategy.

Outside of work, I speak at conferences about AI-native and cloud-native
architecture, DevOps, and open source, and I've spent years supporting the
developer community in Serbia. Most of what I know, I learned from people who
shared it freely. I try to do the same.
